## Findings

Two major findings and seven minor findings. The physics of the Slotboom form, the BiCGSTAB loop and the
backward-error flag are correct, and all 38 tests pass on the GPU computer. The device path fails at moderate
reverse bias, and a README table has numbers that no committed row supports.

1. **major** - `charge_gpu/src/charge_gpu/continuity.py:62,217` and `charge_gpu/src/charge_gpu/drift_diffusion.py:404-412`:
   the device continuity path fails at reverse bias of 5 V and more on the 1-D junction of the tests
   (`junction(n=400)`, 1e18 cm^-3, 2.5 nm cells). The SuperLU path converges there. The claim "the psi span must be
   below about 31 V, else ValueError" is not correct: the failure starts at a span of 230 V_T (6 V).
   Evidence (scratch runs on the GPU computer, `dd.solve(dev, vr, 0.0, method="gummel", linear=..., max_iter=40)`):
   - 2, 3 and 4 V reverse: the device path converges (psi 8e-15 to 7e-14 V, n 2e-13 to 2e-12 from Newton; 40, 105
     and 143 BiCGSTAB iterations from the poisson.solve guess). The tests stop at 3 V.
   - 5 V and 10 V reverse, from the poisson.solve guess and from the Newton solution: ValueError "the potential
     span is too large for the Slotboom scaling (float64 range)". The SuperLU Gummel path converges in 1 to 3
     iterations (psi 3e-14 to 9e-14 V, n 3e-13 to 2e-12 from Newton).
   - The step-by-step run at 5 V from the Newton solution: in Gummel iteration 1, the electron or hole solve stops
     at backward error 1.9e-8 (113 BiCGSTAB iterations, with the 3 restarts), so `linear_converged` is False.
     `_gummel` uses this result. In iteration 2, n is 1e-300 to 2.0e31 m^-3 and the backward error is 1.0. The
     next Poisson step gives a psi span above 1200 V_T, and `Solver.operator` raises the ValueError. So the
     message tells the user about a float64 range limit, but the cause is a linear solve that did not converge.
   - 25 V reverse from the Newton solution (span 1004 V_T, below SPAN_MAX): 1000 Gummel iterations, 8000 BiCGSTAB
     iterations (4 per solve: a breakdown in the first iteration of each restart), backward error 1.0,
     converged = False after about 15 minutes. The flag is correct, but the loop does not stop.
   - Reverse bias is a main use of charge-gpu (depletion modulators, README line 4). The README gives no range for
     `linear="device"`.
   Fix:
   - In `_gummel`, stop at the first device solve that is not converged. Raise RuntimeError (then `sweep` halves the
     step), or solve that step with SuperLU and count the fallbacks in the stats.
   - Correct the 31 V claim. In the README section of `continuity.py` and in Known limits, give the tested range
     (forward to 1.0 V; reverse to 4 V on the 1e18 cm^-3 junction).
   - Add a test at 5 V reverse that checks the new behavior (no ValueError about the span).
   - Optional: find why BiCGSTAB stalls in strong drift (about 5 V_T per cell). Possible fixes: more restarts with
     a new shadow residual, GMRES, or a line smoother.

## What the reviewer tried to break

- **Test suite on the GPU computer** (`bash tools/gpu_run.sh charge_gpu python -m pytest -q -x`): 38 passed in 65.8
  s. The slowest device test is the short-base diode (5.1 s).
- **The Slotboom identity, by hand.** Electrons: B(-d) E_a = exp(d) B(d) E_a = B(d) E_b, so j_n = W (u_b - u_a) with
  W = c B(d) E_b = c B(-|d|) exp((min psi - ref) / V_T). Holes: B(d) F_a = B(-d) F_b, so j_p = W (v_a - v_b) with
  W = c B(-|d|) exp(-(max psi - ref) / V_T). The rows of `_continuity_matrix` give M E = -S (electrons) and
  M_p F = S_p (holes). The contact diagonal (cc B(-dc) E, cc B(dc) F), the recombination diagonal and
  b = -rhs / rhs agree with `Solver.operator`. The test of the operator checks this to 1e-13. I found no sign
  error.
- **The backward-error flag.** |M| |n| = |S| E^-1 E |u| = |S| |u|, and |rhs - M n| = |b - S u|, so the flag is the
  componentwise backward error of the SG system. The host recomputation agrees (3.2e-15 and 7.9e-15 against 3.2e-15
  and 7.8e-15 on the device). With 1 iteration and no restart, the flag is False (test).
- **The BiCGSTAB loop** against the right-preconditioned van der Vorst algorithm: the steps agree. Breakdown cases:
  s_done and tt == 0 give omega 0. A non-finite step keeps the last x and ends the loop. The stall counter and
  the restarts work. With a breakdown at iteration 1 of each restart (25 V), a restart repeats the same breakdown.
  Finding 1 covers this.
- **The overflow guard.** E and F stay in exp(+-600). The products W u and diag u are at most about c n, so they do
  not overflow. The guard is correct for overflow. But the solver fails long before the guard (finding 1).
- **The bounding-box grid.** I checked the index formulas of the x-face grid (bx - 1, by) and of the y-face grid
  (bx, by - 1). Faces exist only between two semiconductor cells. Inactive cells have no faces, diagonal 1 and a
  zero residual and correction. Contact terms enter through the semiconductor index. The 1-D case (by = 1) works.
  Measured: test rib of 2450 cells, box 200 x 22, as the README says.
- **README numbers of the device table**, measured again: one continuity solve takes 11 and 12 iterations, with
  backward error 3.2e-15 and 7.8e-15 and carriers 7.2e-13 and 1.8e-13 off. PIN 0.9 and 1.0 V: 31 and 37 Gummel
  iterations; psi 5.2e-13 and 2.2e-12 V; n 1.2e-11 and 7.8e-11; p 1.4e-11 and 8.8e-11; currents 4.0e-11 and
  2.3e-11; columns 6.3e-11 and 2.2e-11; rib n 2.20e17 and 6.27e17 cm^-3. All agree with the README. The rib index
  change at 1.0 V was 9.0e-11 (README 3.6e-11; test 1e-8). Junction 0.6 V: psi 7.5e-15 V, n 2.4e-13, p 4.2e-13,
  current 5.5e-8 (all agree). The reverse-bias and 0 V values change from run to run (finding 6).
- **The change of the Poisson multigrid** (1024-cell plain pinv changed to a 256-cell scaled pinv):
  - Results: identical digits at 0.6 V and 1 V on the junction. At 0 V the change is at the 1e-14 V and 1e-10 A/m
    level (finding 5).
  - Cost: the Poisson time per Gummel iteration went from 0.435 s to 0.098 s at 10 nm, 0.9 V, and from 0.96 s to
    0.40 s at 5 nm, 0.9 V. The CG iterations stayed about the same (1238 against 1225; 2172 against 1672). So the
    claim that the dense coarsest pinv cost most of the time is correct.
  - The README marks the v1 table as an earlier version, and its numbers are not changed.
- **The full table of the README** (lines 367-372): all 16 times agree with the JSONL rows, to rounding. The small
  table does not (finding 2).
- **Shockley short-base reference**: W_dep = sqrt(2 eps (V_bi - V - 2 V_T) / q * 2 / N), W' = 10 um - W_dep / 2, and
  J = I / height. The units are consistent. The tolerance (1e-3) is about 6x above the Newton deviation (1.7e-4).
- **Option checks**: `linear="cg"` and `newton` + `device` raise ValueError (test). `sweep` passes `linear` through

- **Hygiene**: the patch has no host names, IP addresses, private paths, e-mail addresses, client data or PDK data.

## Fixes by the author
Fixes made by the author agent of the change on my request (tests on the GPU computer only), checked and staged by
the lead agent; the lead agent ran the bench again on the GPU computer with the final code and replaced
benchmarks/2026-10-30/charge-dd-device.jsonl and the README table with the new rows.

1 (major, 5 V reverse and above). Fixed, one root cause: the coarsest multigrid level was inverted with a
pseudo-inverse (accurate to about 1e-16 of the largest entry); across a reverse-biased junction the true entries are
far smaller, and the noise drove BiCGSTAB to diverge. charge_gpu/src/charge_gpu/continuity.py inverts the scaled
M-matrix with LU (pseudo-inverse only if LU fails), returns the vector of the smallest residual, and raises
SpanError with the real span and the limit. drift_diffusion.py does not use a device solve that did not converge or
raised SpanError: that step is solved with SuperLU and counted in linear_fallbacks. The junction converges at 3, 5,
10 and 25 V reverse (backward error about 2e-16); the PIN rib at -5 V converges; tests at 5, 10 and 25 V reverse
(Newton reference by continuation) and a fallback test (a forced failed solve, SPAN_MAX below the span). The README
gives the tested range (1.0 V forward to 25 V reverse) and the limit (a span of 1200 V_T).

and of the limits comes from the logged rows (the new run).

5. Fixed: the 0 V, 0.6 V and 1 V SuperLU-Gummel numbers were measured again with the new Poisson multigrid (0 V:
