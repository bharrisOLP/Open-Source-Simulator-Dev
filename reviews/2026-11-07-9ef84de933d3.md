## Findings
No blocker and no major finding. The periodic copies are exact (they agree to 0.0 with explicit copies of the
objects), the logged rows of 46da80a stay valid, the formulas of the two diagonals are correct, and each README
number that the change touched agrees with the logged rows. 27 tests pass on the GPU computer. Minor findings:

1. minor - fdtd_gpu/README.md:99 ("as in the MIT package") and fdtd_gpu/src/fdtd_gpu/simulation.py:218-221. The
   MIT package repeats the objects by the lattice vectors when k_point is set (`ensure_periodicity`, default True).
   Its rule has no PML condition, so the objects also repeat along an axis with a PML. This change repeats them only
   along axes without a PML. Also, `periodic_shifts` takes every copy that touches the cell, not only the copies of
   the neighbor periods. Both choices are acceptable (a copy inside a PML has a small effect; a waveguide that ends
   in a PML would get a false copy in the MIT package). But the README says "as in the MIT package" and does not
   give the difference. Action: write one sentence in "Differences" (no repeat along a PML axis).
2. minor - fdtd_gpu/src/fdtd_gpu/simulation.py:219 ("as in _build"). For a PML on one side only (for example
   `PML(t, direction=X, side=High)`) with `k_point=Vector3()`, `_build` gives the low x face the type "periodic".
   fdtdx then wraps the whole x axis (`get_wrap_padding_axes` in fdtdx/fdtd/update.py sets wrap when any boundary
   of the axis wraps). But `_periodic_axes` returns False for x, so the objects do not repeat there. This
   configuration is unusual. Action: correct the docstring, or reject k_point with a PML on one side only.
3. minor - fdtd_gpu/tests/test_averaging.py:139-162 (`test_periodic_faces_repeat_objects`). No material face of
   the test lies between the last and the first cell of the periodic axis: in cases c and d the faces are at
   x = +/-0.143 um, and the material is continuous across x = +/-0.25 um. Thus the test also passes with
   mode="edge" in `interface_voxels`, and the new wrap padding has no test. The reviewer checked the wrap padding
   separately (see the next section: a block from x = 0 to 0.26 um in the 0.5 um period). Action: add a block with
   a face between the last and the first cell, and compare it with the same block moved by whole cells
   (np.roll of the arrays).
4. nit - fdtd_gpu/README.md:80 "larger errors at res 40". The forward diagonal gives -0.052 % for m = 8, and the
   inverse diagonal gives +0.061 %. Only m = 9 and 10 have larger errors (-0.112 % and -0.167 % against 0.000 % and
   -0.054 %). Action: write "larger errors at res 40 for m = 9 and 10".
5. nit - fdtd_gpu/tests/test_averaging.py:198 "a mask of up to (11.9 - 1) / 2". The step of E_x and E_y in
