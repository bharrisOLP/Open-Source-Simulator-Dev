## Findings

No blocker and no major finding. Minor findings:

1. minor: charge_gpu/README.md:379 (row "the same, 0.5 V forward and 1 V reverse"). The row gives round-off
   differences of one run as exact values. The reviewer run of the same test (GPU computer, 2026-11-08) printed other
   values: psi 3.9e-15, 2.6e-15, 2.8e-16 V (0.5 V) and 1.1e-14, 9.1e-15, 2.4e-15 V (1 V); n and p 5.9e-13 or better;
   current differences 2.7e-11, -1.6e-10, -1.3e-11 A/m (README: -1.6e-10, -2.4e-16, 7.6e-19 A/m). All are far below
   the test limits, so the claim of agreement holds. A reader who runs the test again gets other digits. Fix: give
   bounds (for example psi <= 1.2e-14 V, n and p <= 8e-13, current <= 2e-10 A/m) and say that these differences are
   round-off and change from run to run.
2. minor: charge_gpu/src/charge_gpu/temperature.py:10-11, charge_gpu/README.md:230-231. The accepted range is 250
   to 500 K, but the n_i fit is measured only to 340 K, and the check of the extrapolation stops at 400 K. The
   band-gap form of the patch itself (bc -l, Thurmond gap, constant masses) differs from the fit ratio by -9.0 % at
   450 K and -11.3 % at 500 K. The form of Couderc, Amara and Lemiti, J. Appl. Phys. 115, 093705 (2014) (the
   reviewer's reading of its parameters: 1.541e15 T^1.712 exp(-E_g0 / 2kT), Paessler gap) differs in the other
   direction: +7 % at 400 K, +13 % at 500 K. So above 400 K n_i has an uncertainty of about 10 %, and the
   currents that scale with n_i^2 have about 20 %. Fix: state this spread in the docstring and the README (or
   extend the band-gap row of the check table to 500 K with its values).
3. minor: charge_gpu/README.md:249-250, charge_gpu/src/charge_gpu/temperature.py:28-31. The list "Not modeled"
   does not name complete ionization of the dopants (no freeze-out). The model assumes full ionization at all T.
   The ionized fraction of 1e17 to 1e18 cm^-3 is below 1 and changes with T, most at 250 K. Fix: add "complete
   ionization of the dopants at every T" to both lists.
4. minor: charge_gpu/src/charge_gpu/temperature.py:19-24, charge_gpu/README.md:238-242. The SRH law
   tau ~ T^-1/2 is the simplest textbook argument (constant capture cross section). Measured SRH lifetimes in Si
   can increase or decrease with T, because capture cross sections depend on T. Also, the 1 ns of the SOI rib is an
   effective lifetime with surface recombination, not a bulk midgap trap. The README PIN numbers at 330 and 360 K
   include this choice (tau 0.953 and 0.913 ns): in high injection at a fixed bias, I is about proportional to
   1/tau, so the choice is about 5 % and 10 % of the current at 330 and 360 K. The text gives the model, but it does
   not say that the exponent is an assumption. Fix: one sentence that the exponent is an assumption, with the size
   of its effect on the example (or a row with tau_exponent=0).
5. minor: charge_gpu/src/charge_gpu/poisson.py:67-74 (changed behavior). Device.n_i now means the 300 K value.
   Before this change, a caller with Device.T != 300 K and a user mobility got n_i as given; now n_i is scaled
   again (a double scaling if the caller already gave n_i at T). poisson.solve also refuses T outside 250 to 500 K
   (before: any T with a constant n_i, which was physically wrong). No test or example in the repository used
   T != 300 K before, and the new docstrings state the meaning. Fix: one line in the README that names this
   change of meaning of Device.n_i.
6. minor (code): charge_gpu/src/charge_gpu/drift_diffusion.py:75 imports `mobility` from temperature at module
   level, and `_mesh` (line 167) and `solve` (line 606) have a parameter with the same name `mobility`. It works now,
   because only `default_mobility` calls the module function. A later edit in `_mesh` or `solve` that calls
   `mobility(...)` would call the user tuple. Fix: `from charge_gpu import temperature` and call
   `temperature.mobility`.
