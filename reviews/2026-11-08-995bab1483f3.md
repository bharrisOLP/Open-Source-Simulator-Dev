1. minor - optics_gpu/src/optics_gpu/pop.py:882-893 (`_auto_nufft`) and optics_gpu/README.md:353-357 (the rule
   table). The rule does not depend on tol. Its limits come from rows at the default tol of each dtype (1e-12 for
   complex128, 1e-6 for complex64). The API accepts any tol from 1e-12 to 1e-2 with method "auto". With
   dtype=torch.complex64 and tol=1e-12, the non-uniform FFT (w = 15) is slower than the direct complex64 sum in
   10 of the 48 logged rows where "auto" selects it, by up to 1.56x (row 108 of optics-nufft-auto.jsonl: 4f, 7713
   rays, 128 x 128, 21 planes: nufft_s_1e-12 0.210 ms against direct_c64_s 0.135 ms per plane). The result is
   then more accurate than the direct complex64 sum, so this is not a wrong result. Fix: in the docstring of
   `_auto_nufft` and in the README, state that the limits hold for the default tol of each dtype. Alternatively,
   scale AUTO_WORK by (w / w_default)^2.
