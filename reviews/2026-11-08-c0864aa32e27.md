## Findings
1. **major**, fdtd_gpu/README.md:194-196 ("Media in the PML"): the remedy "There let the medium end before the PML" is
   wrong for the case that the paragraph describes. Above the plasma edge the wave in the Drude medium propagates
   (that is why it reaches the PML). If the medium ends before the PML, the wave meets a medium-vacuum face. The
   logged rows give its Fresnel reflectance: 0.53 at 1.0 / um (n = 0.162 + 0.154i), 0.082 at 1.2 / um and 0.034 at
   1.38 / um (power). The PML reflection that the remedy removes is 1.25e-2, 2.2e-4 and 2.4e-5 in amplitude
   (1.6e-4, 4.7e-8 and 5.8e-10 in power). The remedy is thus 3 to 7 orders of magnitude worse. When the medium absorbs
   the wave before its end, the PML reflection is already small (the round trip includes exp(-4 pi f Im(n) L)), so
   the remedy does not help in any case. The parenthesis (a thicker PML does not help) is correct, and the package
   has no option for the PML strength (`PML(**unsupported)` raises), so the user has no remedy now.
   Fix: remove the sentence. State the limit: in a medium with a small Re(n) the PML sends back about
   1e-6^Re(n) of the wave that reaches it (times the loss of the medium in the PML); a thicker PML does not change
   this; ending the medium before the PML is worse for a propagating wave (give the 0.53 above). Optionally give a
   real remedy (a PML strength option that sets the fdtdx `sigma_end`, for example R_asymptotic of the MIT package).
2. **minor**, fdtd_gpu/src/fdtd_gpu/simulation.py:617-621 (`_build`, where the lossy media are found): no run-time

   media that touch the PML and the source band, so it can warn when 1e-6^Re(n(f)) is above, for example, 1e-3
   (Re(n) below 0.5) at a source frequency. Fix: add the warning and a test (the Drude half-space of check 14 must
   warn; the Lorentz and conductive ones must not), or make a ticket for it.
3. **minor**, fdtd_gpu/README.md:171-172 and fdtd_gpu/src/fdtd_gpu/_dispersion.py:18-19: the citation "Taflove and
   Hagness, Computational Electrodynamics, 3rd ed., Sec. 3.12" for the semi-implicit lossy update looks wrong. The
   comment in `fdtdx.fdtd.update.update_E` cites "Schneider, chapter 3.12": that is Sec. 3.12 "Lossy Material" of
