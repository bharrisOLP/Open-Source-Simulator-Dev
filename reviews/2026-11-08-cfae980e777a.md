## Findings
No blocker and no major finding. All numbers of the new README section agree with the 18 logged rows (recomputed
below). The findings are about the size of the stated uncertainties and about wording.

1. minor, suite/README.md (section "EME against 3-D FDTD", bullet "Convergence"): "the FDTD limit is known to about
   2e-3" uses the size of the second-order correction as the uncertainty, but the rows do not fix the order. The
   ratio of the 40-50 and 50-60 steps is 5.49, 3.62, 2.62, 1.99, 1.59 over the band (first order gives 1.50, second
   order 1.84), so at 1.60 um the last step looks almost first order. A first-order extrapolation from res 50 and 60
   gives 0.9074 at 1.55 um (second order: 0.9090) and 0.9140 at 1.60 um (second order: 0.9161; 0.0037 below res
   60). Fix: give the FDTD limit as a band (for example 0.907 to 0.909 at 1.55 um, first to second order), or "about
   2e-3 to 4e-3". The conclusions do not change: the EME spread is 0.02 to 0.03, and the FDTD value stays between
   the 20- and the 30-mode EME values for both extrapolations.
2. minor, suite/README.md (bullets "Power" and "Convergence") and suite/src/photonsuite/fdtd_avg.py:415-424 (module
   text, "Mode coefficients"): the calibration makes |a|^2 / b equal to the Yee-grid flux of the mode, and that flux
   has its own O((k n dx)^2) error (fdtd_gpu README: "The flux of the Yee grid is high by O((k dx)^2)"), which is
   larger for the 2.0 um guide (n_eff 2.82) than for the 0.5 um guide (2.45). The logged T_flux at 1.55 um, 1.00265,
   1.00127, 1.00056, 1.00015 at res 30 to 60, fits 1 + 3.0 / res^2 - 0.0007 (within 7e-5 at res 40 and 50). So the
   calibrated T00 carries a normalization bias of about +0.3 % at res 30 and +0.08 % at res 60: about one third of the
   change of T00 from res 30 to 60 (0.0068). The README names only the Yee index error as the probable cause of the
   grid dependence. Fix: one sentence (T00 is per Yee flux; the differential flux error is visible in T_flux > 1 and
   goes to 0 at second order; the extrapolation includes it).
3. minor, suite/README.md (bullet "Power"): "the taper radiates little; the rest of the power is in the higher
   modes" is more than the rows show. The output plane covers the whole window 0.5 um after the taper, so radiation
   that is still inside the window there counts in T_flux; T_flux near 1 shows only that little power leaves through
   the side PML between the two planes. Only TE0 is measured ("Limits": TE2 not compared). Fix: "the rest of the
   power crosses the output plane (higher modes, and radiation that has not left the window yet; not separated)".
4. minor, suite/README.md (bullet "The EME is limited by its interfaces"): the gain of 0.8 to 1.2 % with "none" is
   shown by the logged P_right (1.0080 to 1.0122), and the propagation of real-index modes is lossless, so the
   interfaces are the source: that part is evidence, not a guess. The cause "with a truncated set of modes" is an
   interpretation (it is also the interpretation in the meow docstring of enforce_passivity): the excess falls only
   from 1.2 % to 0.8-0.9 % from 10 to 40 modes, and other causes are not excluded (for example the Gram-Schmidt step
   of meow_gpu.post_process_modes, which keeps the eigenmode neff for a mixed field, so the propagated basis is not
   an exact eigenbasis). Fix: write "probable cause", as for the FDTD grid statement, or add a check (the singular
   values of each interface S-matrix against the mode count).
5. minor, suite/README.md (tables and text): small differences to the rows. FDTD time at res 50 "86.2 s", the row
   has 86.1. EME times "198 s at 30, 300 s at 40", the rows have 199.0 and 296.5. "the FDTD slope changes with the
   grid (0.0013)": that is res 30 against res 60; over res 30 to 60 the slope is 0.0156, 0.0165, 0.0173, 0.0169
   (range 0.0017). The EME curves are not monotonic at 1.575 um (for example "invert" 20 modes 0.8791 at 1.55, 0.8784
   at 1.575), so an end-point slope hides a dip (probably a change of the truncated mode set). Fix: copy the row
   values; give the range 0.0017; mention the dip.
6. minor, suite/tests/test_fdtd_avg.py:1-3: the module docstring says that the file tests that "the taper of
   bench/eme_fdtd_taper.py approaches the EME"; no such test exists (4 tests: staircase map, area of the average,

7. minor, suite/src/photonsuite/fdtd_avg.py:480-484 (ModeSpectrum.input_mode_share): the docstring says "below 1",
   but the logged values are 1.00006 to 1.00018 at 1.525 to 1.575 um (all four resolutions). Fix: "near 1 (within
