## Findings

No blocker and no major finding. Six minor findings:

1. minor: suite/src/photonsuite/pin.py:289-291 and suite/README.md:366 say "T_1 = G(300 K) ... (step 1 is the
   one-pass feedback)". The code (pin.py:329) applies the damping also to the first step:
   T_1 = 300 K + damping (G(300 K) - 300 K). The test print of the damping test shows it (damping 0.5 at 0.9 V:
   residuals 8.0e-1, then 4.1e-1). So with --damping < 1 the bench field `I_one_pass_rel`
   (suite/bench/pin_self_heating.py:115, "the one-pass current of step 1") is not the one-pass feedback. The logged
   rows use damping 1, so no logged number is wrong. Fix: write "T_1 = G(300 K) for damping 1, else
   300 K + damping (G(300 K) - 300 K)", or apply the damping only from step 2; label `I_one_pass_rel` as valid for
   damping 1 only (or compute it from an undamped first step).
2. minor: suite/src/photonsuite/pin.py:292-293, "damping < 1 is for a gain near 1", is wrong. For a gain G' in
   (0, 1) (the feedback here is positive: a hotter device carries more current), the damped map has the rate
   1 - damping (1 - G'), which is larger than G': damping only slows the loop (the test: 14 solves against 3). Damping
   helps for a negative gain (oscillation); for G' >= 1 (thermal runaway) no damping converges. Fix: replace the
   sentence with that statement.
3. minor: suite/README.md:568, "The two parts grow by different amounts". The logged rows show that the carrier
   change falls in size with the two-way coupling at 1.25 and 1.3 V at 1 ns (-0.74 % and -2.39 % of
   dneff_carrier) and at 1.3 V at 10 ns (-1.96 %), while the current rises (the lifetime tau ~ T^-1/2 and the lower
   mobility win over the higher current). The comment of suite/tests/test_pin.py:388 ("the feedback raises ... the
   carrier change") is true only for the tested 0.9 to 1.2 V. Fix: say in the bullet that the carrier part grows up to
   1.2 V (1 ns) and 1.25 V (10 ns) and falls above; limit the test comment to the tested biases.
4. minor: the weight of the device temperature. The README (suite/README.md:586-596) gives the spread of the field
   (8 % to 13 % of the mean rise) but not its effect on the results. The reviewer measured it at 1.15 V (the largest
   change), at the converged field, with one solve per weight (reviewer script, below): the I' change against
   one-way is +7.153 % with the area mean (the loop), +7.155 % with the recombination weight, +7.194 % with the
   heat weight, +7.135 % with the mean of the intrinsic zone, and +6.740 % to +7.529 % at the min and the max of the
   Si (1 ns); at 10 ns +11.892 %, +11.850 %, +12.184 %, +11.753 %, and +11.437 % to +12.834 %. So the plausible
   weights move the change by at most 0.04 (1 ns) and 0.29 (10 ns) percentage points, less than the stated miss of
   the one pass (0.46 and 1.23 points); the hard bound (min to max of the field) is +-0.4 and +-0.7 points. The
   mean is a sound choice, and the README claims stay inside these bounds, but the README does not say so. Fix: add
   these numbers (or the min/max bracket) to the two-way bullets or to the "Coupling" limit.
5. minor: charge_gpu/src/charge_gpu/drift_diffusion.py:639: `area=m.vol.reshape(m.shape)` is a view of the mesh of
   the solution (np.shares_memory is True), while the index arrays are copies. An in-place change of `f.area`
   changes `sol.mesh.vol`. Also, a frozen dataclass with ndarray fields (line 530) has a generated `__eq__` and
   `__hash__` that fail on arrays. Fix: `area=m.vol.reshape(m.shape).copy()`; optionally `eq=False`.
6. minor: no test runs `pin_phase_shifter(coupling="two-way")` to the end (suite/src/photonsuite/pin.py:531-533 passes
   ten positional arguments to `electrothermal`; the order is correct now, by reading, but a change of the signature
   would not be found). The two-way paths with `heat=<tags>` and with `side_temperature` are also not tested. Also, a
   `mobility=` option is refused only after the 300 K sweep. Fix: a one-bias test of
   `pin_phase_shifter(..., coupling="two-way")` against `electrothermal` + `pin_optical`; check the options before
   the sweep.
