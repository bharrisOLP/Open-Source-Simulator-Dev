- Yee positions. In fdtdx 0.6.3, curl_E uses forward differences and curl_H backward differences
  (core/physics/curl.py). Thus E_x is at (i+1/2, j, k), E_y at (i, j+1/2, k) and E_z at (i, j, k+1/2) relative to
  the same index, as the change says; the update multiplies E_a by inv_permittivities[a]. Reviewer test (check 6,
  Hz, res 40, every voxel sampled): voxel of E_a at the cell center + dx/2 (this change): -2.00e-4; at the cell
  center: +7.1e-4; at the center - dx/2: +4.9e-3 (worse than the staircase, -4.0e-3). So the change uses the
  correct position. 1-D cell (layer of eps 4, thickness 0.337 um, res 20): E_x 2.110 (fill 0.370, arithmetic) and
  E_z 2.878 (fill 0.87, harmonic) at the expected z indices; get_epsilon has shape (40,).
- Formula. Kottke et al. (PRE 77, 036611) and Farjadpour et al.: eps_eff = P / <1/eps> + (1 - P) <eps> with
  P = n n^T, so eps_eff^-1 = P <1/eps> + (1 - P) / <eps>, and (eps_eff^-1)_aa = n_a^2 <1/eps> + (1 - n_a^2) / <eps>.
  _inv_eps_chunk computes exactly this. The sign of the normal has no effect (n_a^2). See finding 5 for the name.
- Sampling. 610 and 987 are coprime, and 1021 is prime, so each 1-D projection of the lattice is a permutation of
  the n midpoints, and the symmetric copy gives the same set again: the fill fraction of a plane normal to an
  axis is exact to 1 / (2 n), as test_lattice_projections_and_symmetry checks. The normal grid is mirror
  symmetric, so the moment of a plane normal to an axis is along that axis (the two parallel components are
  equal in test_plane_normal_to_axis); the subtraction of the mean has no effect on the moment.
- Voxels that are not sampled. The check of 2 x 3 (x 3) cell centers covers each voxel. For a planar face, a
  half-space that cuts the hull of these centers contains one of them, so no voxel is lost. A convex curved face
  can enter the hull without a center by a sagitta of about 5 dx^2 / (8 R) (0.016 dx for R = 1 um at res 40):
  small. A feature thinner than one cell that holds no cell center is lost (documented). Reviewer test: sampling
  every voxel gave the same slab frequency to all printed digits.
- fdtdx internals. _init_arrays (StaticMultiMaterialObject without subpixel_smoothing) computes
  perm = perm_slice + mask * (component - perm_slice) with no clip of the mask, and the SimulationVolume is the
  first static object, so perm_slice is eps_bg. fdtdx is pinned (fdtdx==0.6.3), and
  test_fdtdx_gets_the_components_and_get_epsilon fails if the blend changes. The fdtdx mode solver accepts 3
  components (core/physics/modes.py); the 3-D strip gives T_mode 0.9979 to 1.00003.
