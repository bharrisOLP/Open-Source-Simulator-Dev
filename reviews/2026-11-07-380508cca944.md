3. minor - charge_gpu/README.md:318 (the 3 V row) and charge_gpu/tests/test_newton_device.py:232-236 (comment).
   The reason given for the 1e-9 A/m floor is incomplete. The face terms are correct: 9.2e4 A/m at the contacts
   (measured). But double-precision round-off of one such term is 2e-11 A/m, not 1e-9. Measured at 3 V (SuperLU,
   400 cells): the p contact carries 4.28e-11 A/m, the same as q times the integral of G (4.263e-11 A/m from
   `srh` and `svol`); the n contact (`current`) carries -2.96e-10 A/m. The difference (-2.53e-10 A/m) is the sum
   of q F_n, the electron-continuity residual of the converged Newton solution (per cell up to 1.3e-9 A/m). The
   spread of `x_currents()` is 1.5e-9 A/m. So the measured noise supports a floor of about 1e-9 A/m, but its source
   is the residual of the converged Newton solution (about 60 eps of the face terms), not only the round-off of a
   face. Margin of the new assertion `abs(I) < NOISE`: |I| was 2.2e-10 to 4.4e-10 A/m over 16 perturbed starts and
   two runs (2.3x below NOISE). Fix: word it as "the continuity residual of the converged Newton solution (per cell
   up to about 1e-9 A/m, on face terms of about 1e5 A/m)". Optional: also check the physics value, for example the
   p-contact current against q times the integral of G (`recombination()` times `svol`), which agree to 4e-13 A/m.
4. nit - charge_gpu/src/charge_gpu/drift_diffusion.py:687-693 (sweep). `last` is not cleared when a later trial
