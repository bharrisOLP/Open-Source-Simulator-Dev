1. **major**: `README.md:12` (the `optics_gpu/` row): "image quality: OPD, Zernike fit, FFT and Huygens PSF, MTF
   (against Optiland to 1e-11; faster from a pupil of 512)". The parenthesis is false for the Huygens PSF.
   - `optics_gpu/README.md:364` and `:424-427`, and the rows 7 to 9 of
     `benchmarks/2026-11-08/optics-analysis.jsonl`: `huygens_psf` against Optiland's `HuygensPSF` has the correlation
     0.9984 to 0.9985 and the peak 0.02448 against 0.02686 to 0.02711 (about 9 to 10 % lower). These are two methods
     with different amplitude weights (`optics_gpu/README.md:372-386`). The agreement is not 1e-11.
   - "faster from a pupil of 512" is also not the Huygens result: `huygens_psf` is 6.7x, 23x and 79x faster at the
     pupils 64, 128 and 256 (a different method and ray count).
   - The OPD agrees to 1e-11 only on the GPU path: `optics_gpu/README.md:362` gives GPU 8.9e-16, 4.6e-12, 2.3e-12
     and CPU 9.2e-12, 3.2e-11, 6.9e-12 waves (test tolerance 1e-10). The same applies to `STATUS.md:16` ("a singlet
     against Optiland to 1e-11").
   - Fix: write, for example, "image quality: OPD, Zernike fit, FFT PSF and MTF (against Optiland to 5e-12 on the
     GPU, 3.2e-11 on the CPU; faster from a pupil of 512) and a Huygens PSF (correlation 0.998 with Optiland's)".
