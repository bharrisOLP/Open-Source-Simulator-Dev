1. **minor: the averaged eps of fdtd_gpu is NOT mirror symmetric in z (measured); the README says it is.**
   `bands_gpu/README.md:334-337` ("the grid and the averaged eps (the samples are symmetric about each voxel center)
   are mirror symmetric") and `bands_gpu/tests/hole_slab_fdtd.py:17-19` ("the grid, the averaged eps and the PML are
   mirror symmetric"). Cause: `fdtd_gpu._averaging.lattice(3)` is a rank-1 lattice made *centrally* symmetric
   (`np.concatenate([u, -u])`), not symmetric under z -> -z alone. The E_x and E_y nodes are on the slab faces
   (z = +-0.25 is a cell center at resolutions that are multiples of 4), so in the voxels where the hole wall meets
   a face, eps depends on x, y and z, and a voxel and its mirror voxel get different quadrature errors. Measured
   (scratch, `_permittivity()` of the run_fdtd geometry; 1/eps of each component against its mirror node):
   E_x up to 0.0135 (M and K at 16), 0.0174 (M at 32), 0.0289 (M at 16 with the shift (0.5, 0.25)); E_y up to
   0.0155, 0.0189, 0.0197; 144 to 456 voxels per component; E_z only float32 rounding (1.3e-8 to 3.3e-8; its
   voxels do not straddle the faces). The translations by the primitive vectors are exact (0 voxels differ at M 16,
   K 16 with all 5 translations, M 32, M 16 shifted), so the claim "the weights select k exactly" is correct.
   Effect on the results: none seen (Harminv finds only 3 modes in every row at its 1e-5 singular-value cut; the
   300 a/c run moves band 2 at M by only -2.1e-8, so no unresolved second mode is near it; the asymmetric part
   moves the even frequencies at second order only). Fix: write "mirror symmetric except for the quadrature error
   of the fdtd_gpu sample lattice (centrally symmetric, not mirror symmetric) in the voxels where the hole wall
   meets a face (up to about 0.03 in 1/eps); no odd mode is found".
