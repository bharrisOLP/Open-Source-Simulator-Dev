## Findings

1. **major** - charge_gpu/README.md:118-119: "With a 50 Ohm source, the source sets most of the resistance of a
   0.5 mm device" is false for two of the three dopings. The numbers of the change contradict it. At 2 V the device
   resistance R'/L is 84.6 / 0.5 = 169 Ohm (1e17 cm^-3) and 39.4 / 0.5 = 79 Ohm (3e17 cm^-3), against 50 Ohm of
   the source. Only at 1e18 cm^-3 (19.0 / 0.5 = 38 Ohm) is the source the larger part. The bandwidth stays at 11 to
   12.5 GHz for a different reason: the time constant is R'C' + R_s C' L, and with more doping R'C' decreases
   (10.5, 7.8, 6.2 ps) while R_s C' L increases (3.1, 4.9, 8.2 ps). Fix: replace the sentence with this
   trade-off (the two terms per doping).

2. **major** - charge_gpu/README.md:73-75 (grid check): the check uses one bias (2 V), and at 1e18 cm^-3 that
   bias falls near a zero of an error that oscillates with bias. With the default 10 nm cells of `rib_pn` (the
   cells of the example table), the reviewer measured the change of C' from 10 nm to 5 nm cells at 1e18 cm^-3:
   +0.10 % (0 V), -0.15 % (0.25 V), +1.71 % (0.5 V), -0.57 % (1 V, a table row), +0.92 % (1.5 V), +0.13 % (2 V),
   +0.76 % (3 V), -0.22 % (4 V). R' changes up to 0.039 % (0.5 V), not "less than 0.02 %". The 1-D sentence
   ("with 10 nm cells is 0.6 % off eps / W, with 5 nm cells 0.12 %") is true at 2 V only and gives no bias: the
   reviewer measured 10 nm: +0.65 % (0 V), +2.4 % (0.5 V), -0.65 % (1 V), +0.60 % (2 V), +1.25 % (3 V); 5 nm:
   +0.37 %, +0.23 %, +0.17 %, +0.12 %, +0.08 %. Cause: the Debye length (4 nm) is smaller than the cell, and the
   depletion edge moves about 13 nm/V on each side, so it crosses cells as the bias changes (dv = 0.1 V instead of
   0.02 V changes C' by 0.3 % or less, so the central difference is not the cause). At 3e17 and 1e17 cm^-3 the
   error is smooth and small (0.27 % and 0.09 % at 0 V, less at higher bias). Fix: give the worst case over 0 to
   4 V for each doping (about 1.7 % for C' at 1e18 cm^-3 with 10 nm cells), say that the error oscillates with
   bias when the cell is larger than the Debye length, and either use 5 nm cells for 1e18 cm^-3 in the example or
   mark the 1e18 cm^-3 C' values as +-1 %.

3. **major** - charge_gpu/src/charge_gpu/rc.py:110-114: the guard of `series_resistance` raises "needs one
   lateral junction", but it classifies each column only by the sign of its net doping and checks only that the
   n and the p columns are contiguous. A column with both n and p cells passes. Reviewer test: `rib_pn(3e23)`
   with the slab junction at x = +0.15 um and the rib junction at x = 0 (an L-shaped junction): no error,
   `converged` True, R' = 0.989 Ohm m (989 Ohm mm, 25 times the 39.4 Ohm mm of the lateral junction). The
   README (Known limits) says that L-shaped and vertical junctions are not supported, but the code returns a
   silently wrong value. Fix: also require that each semiconductor cell of an n column has n_net > 0 and each
   cell of a p column has n_net < 0 (else ValueError), and add a test with a mixed column.
